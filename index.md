# CheckTrip — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 4, 2026**

**Language:** English | [Português](pt-br.html) | [Español](es.html)

## 1. About CheckTrip

CheckTrip is an Android application designed to help users organize trips, travelers, reservations, expenses, checklists, and travel documents.

This Privacy Policy explains how CheckTrip handles information stored on users' devices and how integrated third-party services may process certain data.

CheckTrip is designed to operate primarily offline, without requiring an account for its core features.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on the features you use, CheckTrip may store:

- Trip details, destinations, dates, and itineraries.
- Traveler information, including information about accompanying minors entered by a responsible user.
- Reservations, expenses, and financial records.
- Checklists and tasks.
- Travel documents, photographs, and attachments.
- Application preferences and settings.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device using protected storage mechanisms, including encrypted databases.

CheckTrip does not automatically upload the contents of your trips, documents, or attachments to the developer's servers.

## 3. Device Permissions

CheckTrip may request access to specific device features, depending on the functionality you choose to use.

These may include:

- **Camera and files:** to add photographs, documents, and attachments.
- **Notifications:** to provide reminders and other application notifications.
- **Notification access:** when explicitly enabled, to support features that identify financial information from notifications.
- **Internet and network state:** to support advertising consent and advertising services, when applicable.

Permissions are used for their associated functions. Disabling optional permissions may limit those functions but does not prevent the use of unrelated features.

## 4. Advertising and Google Services

CheckTrip integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising infrastructure and privacy choice management.

Advertising is currently being prepared and tested. Real advertisements are not yet enabled in the development version described by this policy.

When advertising services are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process technical information such as:

- Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.
- IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
- Device characteristics and application information.
- Advertising interactions and diagnostic information.
- Information used for security, fraud prevention, and ad measurement.

Google services may also process technical data during initialization or privacy consent operations, according to their applicable policies.

For more information:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 5. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable laws, CheckTrip uses Google UMP to request and manage privacy choices related to advertising.

Users may be presented with consent forms or other privacy notices. Where required, an option to review privacy choices will be available within the application's settings.

Consent decisions may be processed and retained by Google's consent management services.

Declining optional advertising consent does not prevent access to CheckTrip's core features.

## 6. Sharing and Exporting Information

CheckTrip allows users to manually export, back up, and share information.

When you choose to share information through another application or service, that recipient's privacy practices may apply.

CheckTrip does not automatically share the contents of locally stored travel records with advertising providers.

Technical information required by integrated third-party services may be processed as described in this policy.

## 7. Data Security

CheckTrip uses protective measures that include encrypted local database storage and encrypted backup functionality.

These measures are intended to reduce unauthorized access to stored information.

Users should protect their devices and exported backups. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Travel records and other user-generated content remain locally stored until removed through available application features, cleared from device storage, or deleted through application removal, subject to Android behavior.

Exported backups and copies stored outside the application's private storage are not necessarily deleted when the application is uninstalled.

Data processed by third-party services is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

Users may contact the developer for questions about information processed directly by the developer. The developer cannot directly access or delete information that remains exclusively on a user's device.

## 9. Payments, Subscriptions, and Voluntary Contributions

CheckTrip is preparing optional subscriptions to remove advertising and voluntary contributions through external payment providers.

These payment features are not yet active in the development version covered by this policy.

If introduced, payment processing will be handled through the relevant providers, such as Google Play, Pix payment institutions, or Stripe, as applicable.

CheckTrip is not designed to store full payment card numbers.

This policy will be updated before these services become operational, including any additional personal information processed.

## 10. International Data Processing

Third-party technology providers may process technical information in countries other than the user's country of residence.

Such processing is governed by applicable laws and the providers' respective privacy policies.

Locally stored travel information is not automatically transferred internationally by the developer.

## 11. Children's Privacy

CheckTrip is intended as a general travel organization tool and is not specifically directed at children for independent use.

A responsible user may enter information about children or other travelers when organizing a trip.

The application does not require children to register accounts.

Users should avoid storing unnecessary sensitive personal information and take appropriate measures to protect documents belonging to minors.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the applicable law, including Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), the GDPR, and relevant United States state privacy laws, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request information about the processing of their personal data.

Locally stored information can be managed using available application features.

Privacy choices related to Google advertising services may be managed through the consent tools provided in the application, when applicable.

For questions or requests concerning information processed directly by the developer, contact the address below.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to CheckTrip, integrated services, or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page, together with its update date.
